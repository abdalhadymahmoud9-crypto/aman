Aman Web Filter

The built-in list (90,304 domains, from the StevenBlack/hosts project, MIT license) ships in 4 rule sets: a 28,000-domain core that is always on while filtering is enabled, plus 3 extended sets that Chrome activates when its rule budget allows - the toolbar popup shows how many sets are active. Here you manage your own additions. Allowed always wins over blocked. Everything is stored only in this browser.
