Streak semantics: calendar days the filter VPN has been continuously running,
start day = day 1; pause or any stop resets. "Alive" requires the persisted flag
AND the live in-process service marker AND a monotonic (elapsedRealtime) heartbeat
fresher than 120s - so process kills and reboots never fake a live streak, with no
false-ON window. Conservative note: if the system restarts the sticky service after
a kill, the streak restarts from that day (the VPN did stop).

Rebuild APK: cd android-source && gradle assembleDebug (needs JDK 17 + Android SDK 34)
